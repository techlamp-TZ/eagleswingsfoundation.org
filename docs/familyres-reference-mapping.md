# FamilyRes → Eagle's Wings: Reference-to-Design Mapping

Reference: <https://www.familyres.org/> — Family Residences and Essential Enterprises, Inc.

## What I observed vs. assumed

I inspected FamilyRes through its page text only (no visual/asset access), so I can map its
**information architecture and content structure** with confidence, but not its exact colours,
type scale, photography composition or spacing. Everything below distinguishes **structure
(observed)** from **styling (assumed, therefore not copied)**.

Observed structure:

- Grouped dropdown navigation (`Services` / `About` / `Partners` / `Get Involved`) plus a
  standalone orange **"Donate Now"** button.
- A strong **static hero**: short headline ("Growth. Independence. Community."), a one-line
  supporting statement, and immediate routes into services.
- Large, image-led **service cards** directly under the hero, each with a "Learn More" action.
- An **organisation introduction** with founding year, mission, and a prominent **"Start Here"**
  entry point into programs.
- A **programs grid** (image + title + "Learn More" per program).
- A dated **News & Events** block, a **newsletter** sign-up and a **network/partner logo** strip.
- A **trust footer**: physical address, phone, privacy/legal links, social icons, and a legal
  status note.

## What to adapt (and why it helps Eagle's Wings)

| FamilyRes pattern | Adaptation for Eagle's Wings | Why it helps visitors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grouped dropdown nav + single Donate button | Keep the existing 5-item nav + Resources dropdown + orange Donate button (already grouped around our services) | Single-line navigation scaled to a small organisation |
| Strong static hero with headline + one-line support + routes into services | Keep the split hero ("Inclusion begins with understanding.") with "Find support" as the primary action | Families land and immediately see the path to help |
| "Start Here" entry point | Add a "Start here" label to the five-journey section; `/get-started` stays the hub | A family, caregiver, donor or partner finds their route without reading the whole site |
| Image-led program cards | Add a relevant photo (or an icon tile where no honest photo exists) to each of the five program areas on the home page | Programs feel real and concrete, not abstract |
| News & Events with dates | Keep the existing News/Events pages, which already use honest "planned / not yet published" states | Credibility without inventing activity |
| Trust footer (address, phone, legal) | Keep the existing footer (Tegeta address, phone, WhatsApp, privacy) | Institutional trust in a community-service context |

## What to change (this is a smaller organisation)

- **No large Services mega-menu.** Eagle's Wings has five program areas, so ordinary grouped
  links are enough (already the case).
- **No "Join Our Team" hiring band or US "Careers" framing.** Careers stays a footer link only.
- **No beneficiary figures, US eligibility rules, 501(c)(3) status, or partner logos.** Those are
  FamilyRes's facts, not ours; we publish only what we can substantiate.
- **No newsletter sign-up.** There is no working integration, so we do not add a fake form.
- **Honest empty states stay.** Reports, events and impact stories remain marked "not yet
  published" until verified.

## The five visitor journeys (implemented)

1. **A family looking for support** → Neuro-diversity Awareness, Education & Therapy Guidance, Life Skills.
2. **A caregiver looking for guidance or training** → Caregiver Training, Elderly Care Support.
3. **A donor looking to support the Foundation** → Donate.
4. **A volunteer or partner looking to contribute** → Get Involved, Partners.
5. **A visitor looking for therapy or sensory equipment** → Equipment Catalogue.
