You can get involved with Eagle's Wings Foundation Tanzania by volunteering, donating, exploring a partnership, sharing respectful awareness, or simply getting in touch. This page answers the questions people ask most often and points you to the website pages that hold the up-to-date details.

We are a non-profit founded in 2025 and based in Tegeta, Dar es Salaam. Our work supports people with disabilities and challenges, especially children and older people, and their families and caregivers.

## What are the ways to get involved?

There is no single right way. Here is a simple overview.

| Way to take part | What it involves | Where to start |
|---|---|---|
| Volunteer | Giving time and care, following our guidance | [Volunteers](/volunteers) |
| Donate | Supporting the work financially | [Donate](/donate) |
| Partner | Working together as an organisation | [Contact](/contact) |
| Learn and share | Reading, and sharing respectful information | [Blog](/blog) |
| Ask a question | Getting in touch | [Contact](/contact) |

The [Get Involved](/get-involved) page brings these routes together.

## Can I volunteer, and what will it be like?

The [Volunteers](/volunteers) page describes current opportunities and what we ask of volunteers. Please read it first, because information may change.

A few principles apply to everyone who helps:

- **Dignity first.** People are not "cases" or photo subjects. They are people with the right to be respected.
- **Consent and privacy.** Ask before photographing or sharing anything, and follow what you are told.
- **Safety.** Water and exercise activities need trained supervision. A volunteer should not take on tasks that need a professional.
- **Reliability.** Turning up as agreed matters, especially to children and older people who value routine.

Our related article, [responsible volunteering in community work](/blog/article-responsible-volunteering-in-community-work), explores this in more depth. The UN Volunteers programme also has useful general information about volunteering.

## How can I donate?

The [Donate](/donate) page explains the current ways to give. If anything is unclear, please ask through the contact page before you give. We encourage you to expect honesty from any organisation you support, including us. We describe our values and approach rather than promising particular numbers or outcomes.

## How can I contact the Foundation?

You have several options:

- Use the [Contact](/contact) page on this website.
- Call **+255 749 044 723**.
- Follow **@Eaglewings.tz** on Instagram.
- Visit us in Tegeta, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania (please contact us first).

Please do not send private medical information about someone else in a first message. If you or a family member needs a diagnosis, treatment or urgent help, speak with a doctor, nurse, therapist or social worker.

## What if I want to join as an organisation or partner?

Organisations are welcome to get in touch through the [Contact](/contact) page. We believe partnerships should share values: dignity, inclusion, transparency and safety. Our article on [responsible partnerships with the Foundation](/blog/article-responsible-partnerships-with-the-foundation) covers this in more detail.

## Can I help without giving money or time?

Yes. Awareness matters. You can:

- Learn about disability, autism and dementia from reliable sources such as the World Health Organization.
- Use respectful language, such as "person with a disability" or "person living with dementia".
- Invite a person with a disability to community events, and make sure they can take part comfortably.
- Share our articles with a friend or relative who may find them useful.

The WHO estimates that [about 1.3 billion people](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/disability-and-health), or roughly 16% of the world's population, experience significant disability. In any community, someone near you is likely to be affected, directly or through family.

## What if I have a question about careers or events?

The [Careers](/careers) page is the best place to look for current information. We only announce something there when it is confirmed. For everything else, our [FAQ](/faq) page may already answer your question.

## What should I keep in mind before I reach out?

- Be clear about what you can offer, and about what you are hoping to learn.
- Be patient. We are a young foundation and want to reply carefully.
- Respect privacy, including your own. See our [Privacy](/privacy) page.
- Remember that our articles are educational, and are not medical, legal or clinical advice.

## What this means at Eagle's Wings

We welcome people who share our respect for dignity and inclusion. If you are ready to start, visit the [Get Involved](/get-involved) page, or read our [mission, vision and values](/about#mission) to see whether our purpose feels like a good fit.
